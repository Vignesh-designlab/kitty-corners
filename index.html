How a round works

  • 1A 3-second countdown, then the same board appears for everyone.
  • 2Tap a square to place a cat. Squares it rules out are crossed off for you.
  • 3A wrong cat adds 5 seconds. Stuck? Tap Hint and the coach shows the next move and why.
  • 4Fastest finish wins the round. Out of time? You still score for every cat you placed.
